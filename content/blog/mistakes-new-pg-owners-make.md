Running a PG can be a steady business, but small mistakes in the first year can cost a lot of money and time. Here are nine to avoid.

## 1. No written rules

Without written rules about visitors, timings, noise and notice, every issue becomes an argument. Write the rules, share them, and ask each tenant to sign.

## 2. No agreement or notice period

Even a simple agreement that states the rent, the deposit and the notice period protects you. Verbal promises are hard to prove.

## 3. Mixing the deposit with income

The deposit belongs to the tenant until you have a reason to keep part of it. Keep it separate from the monthly rent in your records.

## 4. Keeping accounts in your head or on loose paper

You will forget who paid what. Use one place for every invoice and payment, with dates and reference numbers.

## 5. Not checking identity documents

Always collect an ID proof and keep it safely. It protects you and the other tenants.

## 6. Ignoring small complaints

A leaking tap becomes a damaged wall. Record every complaint, give it a status and close it.

## 7. Unclear electricity charges

Decide how the electricity is shared, write it down and show the working on each invoice.

## 8. Letting dues grow

A small due is easy to collect. A big due is hard. Remind early and follow up the next day.

## 9. Not filling empty beds quickly

Every empty bed is lost income. Know which beds are vacant, keep the contact details of people who enquired, and follow up with them.
