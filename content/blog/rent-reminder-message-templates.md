A polite, clear reminder gets rent paid faster than an angry one. Copy and change these messages for your tenants.

## Before the due date (2 or 3 days)

**English**

> Hello [Name], this is a friendly reminder that your rent of ₹[Amount] for [Month] is due on [Date]. You can pay by UPI to [UPI ID]. Please send the payment reference after paying. Thank you!

**Hindi**

> नमस्ते [नाम] जी, यह एक छोटा सा रिमाइंडर है कि [महीना] का किराया ₹[राशि] [तारीख] को देय है। आप UPI से [UPI ID] पर भुगतान कर सकते हैं। भुगतान के बाद कृपया रेफरेंस नंबर भेज दें। धन्यवाद!

## On the due date

> Hello [Name], today is the due date for your rent of ₹[Amount]. Please pay when you can and share the reference number. Thank you.

## One day after

> Hello [Name], I have not received your rent of ₹[Amount] for [Month] yet. Please pay today or tell me when you will be able to. Thank you.

## For a repeat late payer

> Hello [Name], your rent for [Month] is now [Number] days late. Please clear ₹[Amount] by [Date]. If there is a problem, please call me so we can talk.

## Tips

- Always include the amount, the month, the date and how to pay.
- Keep the tone calm and respectful.
- Send the invoice as a PDF so the tenant sees the full calculation.
- Write down the date you sent each reminder.
