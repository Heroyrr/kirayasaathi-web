The first day sets the tone for the whole stay. A short, clear process avoids most problems later.

## Before handing over the keys

1. **Collect the documents.** Take a copy of an ID proof such as Aadhaar, a passport photo, and the tenant’s phone number. Ask for an emergency contact.
2. **Sign the agreement.** The tenant should read and sign it, and receive a copy.
3. **Take the deposit and advance.** Write down the amounts, the date and how they were paid, and give a receipt.

## In the room

4. **Check the room together.** Look at the walls, fan, lights, bed, cupboard, taps and any furniture. Note any damage and take photos with the date.
5. **Note the meter reading.** Write the starting reading, and take a photo of the meter.
6. **Hand over the keys** and write down how many you gave.

## Explain the basics

7. **House rules.** Visitors, timings, noise, cleanliness and use of common areas.
8. **How and when to pay.** The due date, the UPI ID or QR code, and how the electricity share is worked out.
9. **How to report a problem.** Tell the tenant to message you, and that you will note it and follow up.

## Then record everything

Create the first invoice with the rent and, if you take it, the deposit. Show any advance that the tenant has paid. A clean record on day one saves arguments on move-out day.
